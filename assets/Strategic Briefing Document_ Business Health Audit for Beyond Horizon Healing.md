# Strategic Briefing Document: Business Health Audit for Beyond Horizon Healing

## Executive Summary

**Beyond Horizon Healing** is a micro-scale holistic health and wellness consultancy based in Caversham (Swan Valley), Perth, Western Australia. Founded and operated by sole practitioner **Jo Flynn**, the business delivers complementary and alternative medicine (CAM) services—including clinical reflexology, Reiki energy healing, and Chakradance workshops—alongside high-ticket retail distribution of zazen alkaline water filtration systems.

An exhaustive, evidence-grounded audit of the organisation’s commercial footprint, regulatory posture, and digital ecosystem yields an overall **DIVE Score of 56/100**. This score reflects a profound internal dichotomy:

1. **Operational Pragmatism:** The business demonstrates lean operational efficiency by leveraging established third-party scheduling platforms (Fresha), maintaining low digital overhead, cultivating local community trust, and successfully integrating high-margin retail products to diversify revenue beyond time-bound clinical services.
2. **Critical Regulatory Vulnerability:** The practice faces immediate, existential threats from federal and state regulatory bodies, including the **Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA)**, the **Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC)**, and the Western Australian **Health and Disability Services Complaints Office (HaDSCO)**. These risks stem from the public use of unapproved therapeutic claims, prohibited medical outcome testimonials, and non-compliance with statutory disclosure mandates.

The non-negotiable priority for Beyond Horizon Healing is aggressive **defensive remediation**. Immediate operational efforts must focus on sanitising public-facing promotional language, complying with Western Australian health care laws, restoring broken contact pathways, and documenting formal practitioner credentials.

## Deep-Dive Dimensional Analysis

### 1. Defence (Score: 35/100) — Critical Exposure

The Defence dimension evaluates structural integrity and protection against legal or regulatory harm. Unregistered health practitioners in Western Australia operate within a strict legislative framework. The audit identified severe, systemic non-compliance across three primary domains.

#### A. Unlawful Therapeutic Claims & TGA Exposure
The organisation’s public digital channels repeatedly use medicalised language that links alternative modalities to the treatment or prevention of serious medical and psychological conditions.

* **Observed Evidence:**
  * **Reflexology Copywriting:** Asserts that chronic stress contributes to "dis eases of the immune system and the development of depression and other mental health issues," positioning reflexology as a key mechanism to clear these underlying blockages.
  * **Homepage Copywriting:** Links modern lifestyle speeds to "cancer and heart conditions," presenting Reiki energy work as essential for clearing negative factors associated with these severe illnesses.
  * **Chakradance Promotional Material:** Claims frequency movement unravels unconscious memories that become "the illnesses we develop," stating explicitly that the practice can "heal and let go unprocessed emotions and trauma."
  * **Retail Claims:** Promotes zazen Alkaline water systems as hydrating at a cellular level and removing toxins, reinforced by client statements that the system "aided hugely in my recovery."
* **Legal Context & Regulatory Impact:**
  * Under the **Therapeutic Goods Act 1989** and the **Therapeutic Goods Advertising Code 2021 (TGAC)**, making references to serious diseases (cancer, heart conditions, depression, immune disorders) constitutes a **restricted representation**. Publishing restricted representations without explicit prior approval from the TGA is a strict liability offence.
  * Claiming a product or service treats or cures a health condition without scientific consensus violates Sections 18 and 29 of the **Australian Consumer Law (ACL)** regarding misleading and deceptive conduct. Regulatory bodies (TGA and ACCC) actively enforce these statutes, with penalties reaching substantial financial sums (e.g., Lorna Jane’s $5M fine for unsubstantiated health claims).
  * High-quality standards from professional bodies like the **Reflexology Association of Australia (RAoA)** specify that reflexology aids relaxation and stress relief, but must never be advertised as a cure or direct treatment for medical conditions.

##### Mandatory Copywriting Transformation Strategy

| Current Uncompliant Language | Legal Risk Profile | Target Compliant Copywriting |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"Reflexology effectively treats Stress and Anxiety... chronic stress has been identified as a contributing factor in many dis eases of the immune system and the development of depression."* | High: Unapproved therapeutic claim; restricted representation of depression and immune illness. | *"Reflexology is a deeply relaxing modality that helps manage everyday tension and promotes a general sense of calm and well-being."* |
| *"Many of today's ailments (from weight gain and mental health issues through to cancer and heart conditions) stem from our increased pace... Reiki... clears the negative."* | Severe: Illegal restricted representations of cancer and heart conditions under the TGAC 2021. | *"Reiki is a gentle energetic practice designed to support relaxation and help you find balance in a fast-paced modern world."* |
| *Chakradance will "heal and let go unprocessed emotions and trauma."* | High: Misleading therapeutic outcome claim regarding trauma and psychological healing. | *"Chakradance is a supportive movement practice that encourages self-exploration, emotional release, and energetic alignment."* |

#### B. Prohibited Use of Outcome-Based Health Testimonials
The business relies on outcome-focused testimonials on its primary homepage to establish clinical efficacy.

* **Observed Evidence:**
  * Review from *"Dimtak"*: *"I had back pain for a while and after the treatment, the pain was gone completely."*
  * Review from *"Mat"*: State the practitioner *"worked wonders on my ankle."*
  * Review from *"Former Client"*: Details histories of pain in the *"elbow, neck, shoulder, hands and knee,"* stating that after sessions they *"feel less pain and as a result, have an improved quality of life."*
  * Review from *"Shannon"* (zazen water): *"Aided hugely in my recovery."*
* **Legal Context & Regulatory Impact:**
  * The 2021 TGAC revisions and guidelines enforced by **AHPRA** and state bodies explicitly prohibit the use of testimonials that contain therapeutic claims or references to physiological outcomes.
  * An individual client’s subjective report (e.g., "the pain was gone completely") does not constitute scientific evidence and creates unverified expectations for consumers seeking medical relief. When a practitioner displays an outcome-based testimonial on their platform, they legally adopt that claim, exposing themselves to prosecution for misleading advertising under the ACL.

##### Testimonial Compliance Model

| Prohibited Outcome-Based Testimonial | Compliant Process/Experience-Focused Alternative |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"I had back pain for a while and after the treatment, the pain was gone completely."* | *"Jo provided a deeply relaxing experience. The clinic space was peaceful, and I left feeling incredibly calm and well-cared for."* |
| *"I have had my second appointment with Jo and I can say she has worked wonders on my ankle."* | *"Jo is extremely knowledgeable and professional. Her approach to reflexology made me feel comfortable and supported throughout the entire session."* |

#### C. Non-Compliance with WA Statutory Code of Conduct
The business fails to disclose mandated statutory information required for unregistered health practitioners operating in Western Australia.

* **Observed Evidence:** The website, Fresha profiles, and collateral contain no references to the **Health and Disability Services Complaints Office (HaDSCO)**, nor do they link to or publish the statutory Code of Conduct.
* **Legal Context & Regulatory Impact:**
  * Effective July 2023, Western Australia enacted the mandatory **Code of conduct for certain health care workers**, binding all non-NRAS health providers (including reflexologists and Reiki practitioners).
  * **Clause 17 Mandate:** Practitioners must prominently display a copy of the Code of Conduct in their physical workspace and provide clear digital links explaining how clients can submit complaints via HaDSCO.
  * Failure to comply exposes the practitioner to HaDSCO investigations, **Interim Prohibition Orders (IPOs)** suspending practice, and fines of up to **$30,000**.

### 2. Identity (Score: 65/100) — Under-Evidenced

The Identity dimension assesses whether the practitioner’s real-world authority, qualifications, and brand narrative are clearly documented.

#### A. Absence of Documented Formal Qualifications
* **Observed Evidence:** The business uses professional titles including *"Clinical Reflexologist,"* *"Reiki Master,"* and *"Chakradance™ Facilitator."* However, the digital properties omit details regarding formal qualifications, granting institutions, graduation dates, or active memberships in governing associations (such as the Reflexology Association of Australia). The "About" section focuses instead on general experiences across legal, mental health, education, and manufacturing sectors, along with self-directed reading.
* **Commercial & Conversion Impact:**
  * High-discernment clients and potential allied health referral sources (GPs, physiotherapists) require objective verification of clinical competence.
  * The prefix *"Clinical"* implies formal tertiary or vocational training (e.g., HLT52515 Diploma of Reflexology). Without visible accreditation, insurance details, or professional association badges, prospective clients face a trust barrier, which suppresses conversion rates.

#### B. Founder Narrative Dynamics
* **Observed Evidence:** The founder’s journey is built around personal grief—the loss of her aunt to cancer—which inspired a 25-year search into holistic health. The narrative explicitly states: *"I wouldn't accept Western medicine's perspective of why her body was so sick."*
* **Strategic Assessment:**
  * **Strength:** Anchoring the practice in shared human experience creates authentic empathy and emotional safety for trauma-impacted clients.
  * **Risk:** Positioning holistic modalities in direct opposition to "Western medicine" creates commercial and regulatory exposure. Adversarial phrasing alienates conventional medical providers, closing off B2B GP referral networks. Furthermore, **Clause 3 of the WA HaDSCO Code of Conduct** prohibits unregistered health care workers from dissuading clients from seeking or continuing conventional medical treatment.

## Foundational Operational Strengths

The audit identifies key operational strategies that are performing well and should be maintained:

1. **Streamlined Scheduling Architecture:** Offloading calendar bookings, payment processing, and SMS reminders to Fresha minimizes administrative overhead for the single operator.
2. **Cohesive Modality Stacking:** Combining physical treatment (Reflexology), energetic alignment (Reiki), and movement (Chakradance) creates a clear, distinct positioning focused on emotional and physical tension release.
3. **Active Community Integration:** Participating in regional industry networks, running workshops, and engaging in peer groups (such as 'The Reflexology Connection' Facebook group) helps prevent clinical isolation and builds professional referrals.
4. **Complementary Retail Revenue:** Incorporating physical health products (zazen water systems) decouples income from manual clinical hours in a way that fits the core brand narrative.

## Regulatory Frameworks & Evidentiary Standards

This briefing document derives its recommendations from public records and active Australian legislative frameworks governing non-allopathic health practices:

* **Therapeutic Goods Advertising Code 2021 (TGAC) & Therapeutic Goods Act 1989:** Establishes strict liability for unapproved therapeutic claims, restricted representations (cancer, cardiovascular diseases, depression), and subjective health testimonials.
* **Australian Consumer Law (ACL) (Competition and Consumer Act 2010):** Sections 18 and 29 prohibit misleading and deceptive conduct, including unsubstantiated health claims.
* **WA Health and Disability Services Complaints Office (HaDSCO):** Governs the *Code of conduct for certain health care workers* (enforceable July 2023). Clause 17 mandates local and digital posting of consumer rights, while Clause 3 regulates care advice and claims.
* **Professional Standards for Reflexology:** Adheres to advertising guidelines published by the Reflexology Association of Australia (RAoA) and Professional Reflexology standards, which require alternative practitioners to emphasize general relaxation and stress reduction over medical cures.
